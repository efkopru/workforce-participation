About the data

A fixed snapshot of monthly state-level labor market indicators from January 2020 through January 2026 (51 jurisdictions: 50 states + DC). Counts are in thousands, seasonally adjusted. The app reads the supplied State-Grid view.csv file and does not fetch live data.

  • Participation rate (LFPR) — share of the civilian 16+ population working or looking for work.
  • “vs Feb 2020” — change versus February 2020, the last full pre-pandemic month (the dataset’s own baseline). Delta series begin in March 2020.
  • Job openings rate — computed here as openings ÷ (employment + openings) × 100, the JOLTS definition.
  • Unemployed per job opening — the dataset’s “Available Worker Ratio”. Below 1.0 means more openings than unemployed people: a labor shortage.
  • Quit / hire rates — quits and hires as a share of employment (JOLTS).
  • October 2025: observations are missing for all 51 jurisdictions in the supplied snapshot. This gap is shown without interpolation.

Static ES-module app — D3 v7 + TopoJSON from CDN, data read straight from the CSV at load. No build step.
